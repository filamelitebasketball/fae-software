How we run
the building.

This is the whole job in one page. If you are new, read it top to bottom once — it takes about ten minutes. After that, use the Quick Card at the bottom; print it and keep it at the desk.

Everything here is the same for everyone. When something goes wrong, nobody should have to guess what the right move was.

01

The one rule

Log it the moment it happens. Not at the end of your shift.

No-show, damage, injury, argument, overtime, a renter who paid differently than agreed — press Log an Incident and write it down while the person is still standing there. A logged incident is a solved incident. An unlogged one becomes your word against theirs a week later.

02

Where records live

We run on several machines. Some things are shared across all of them; some are saved only on the computer in front of you. Getting this wrong is how records go missing, so learn these three:

GOOGLE SHEET
The court schedule

Every booking, for every day. Same on every device, updates instantly. This is the only booking record that counts — never take a renter's word over it.

LIVE SITE
Members, payments, roles, stock

Kept on the F.A.E. website's admin page. Same on every device. Anything another person needs to see later goes here.

THIS DEVICE
Checklist ticks, drinks count, daily task submissions, incident drafts

Saved in the browser on that one computer only. Useful for running your shift. It does not travel to any other machine, and it is lost if the browser data is cleared.

The rule that follows from this

If it must match on every device, it does not live in this folder. Put it on the Sheet or the live site. When in doubt, do both and tell the Master.

03

When a renter arrives

In order, every single time. This sequence is what stops the mix-ups.

Open the live court schedule first

Before anything else, look at today's column. Never take someone's word for their booking.

Match the person to the booking

Name, court, start time, end time. If they are not on the schedule, they are not booked — take the booking properly before they play.

Say the end time out loud

Saying it out loud is what prevents the overtime argument later.

"You're on Court 1 until 4 PM."

Collect payment now, not later

Cash or GCash. Mark it the moment it is in your hand — not after they finish playing.

Check the court before they start

Lights, nets, floor, rubbish. If something is already broken, log it now so it is not blamed on this group.

Watch the clock near the end

Give a 10-minute warning. If they run over, that is overtime — log it as an incident and charge it.

Log anything that goes wrong, immediately

No-show, damage, injury, argument, overtime. See rule 01.

Hand over cleanly

Before you leave: tasks submitted, drinks counted, cash matches, next shift told what happened.

04

The shift, start to finish

Shift blocks give 24-hour coverage: A Morning 06:00–14:00, B Afternoon 14:00–22:00, C Graveyard 22:00–06:00.

OpeningFIRST 15 MINUTES
  1. Open the Command Center and read today's schedule top to bottom.
  2. Open courts — lights, fans, doors.
  3. Count the cash float before you take a single payment.
  4. Fridge count — start of shift, in the Staff Hub.
  5. Clock in on the Staff Hub and check your hours are right.
DuringALL SHIFT
  1. Confirm every renter on arrival against the schedule.
  2. Mark each payment immediately.
  3. Log anything that goes wrong, immediately.
  4. Keep the courts clear between bookings.
  5. Tick daily tasks as you complete them, not in one batch at the end.
ClosingLAST 20 MINUTES
  1. Note no-shows and tomorrow's first booking.
  2. Fridge count — end of shift.
  3. End-of-day cash count. It must match what was marked paid.
  4. Submit your daily tasks — this locks the day under your name.
  5. Secure the court, lock up, tell the next shift what happened.
If the cash does not match

Do not adjust the number to make it fit and do not wait until tomorrow. Log it as an incident with the amount and what you think happened, then tell the Master before you leave. An honest short count is a small problem; a hidden one is a big one.

06

Incidents

An incident is anything you would want proof of later. If you are wondering whether it counts, it counts.

TYPEWHAT TO CAPTURE
No-showName, booked slot, how long you waited, whether they paid.
DamagePhoto before and after if you can, which group was on, estimated cost.
InjuryWho, what happened, what you did, whether they went to a clinic. Tell the Master straight away.
ArgumentWho was involved, what it was about, how it ended. Stay factual, no opinions.
OvertimeBooked end time, actual end time, whether the extra was charged.
Payment problemAmount expected, amount received, method, what was agreed.

Attach a photo where it helps — but keep it to a few small photos so it saves reliably. You do not need the person to be a registered member: type their name in the name box and file it.

When to escalate

NOW

Injury, fire, theft, anyone threatening staff or another renter, police involvement.

NOW

Cash does not match by any amount you cannot explain.

TODAY

Damage to the court or equipment, a refund request, a renter refusing to pay.

TODAY

Double booking, or a booking on the Sheet that nobody can account for.

THIS WEEK

Repeat no-shows from the same renter, stock running consistently short, equipment wearing out.

08

If the computer is off

The building does not stop because a screen does. Fall back to paper and catch up afterwards — that is a normal part of the job, not a failure.

On paper, right awayDO NOT SKIP
  1. Renter name, sport, court.
  2. Start time and end time.
  3. Amount, and cash or GCash.
  4. OR number issued.
  5. Anything that went wrong.
When it is backSAME SHIFT
  1. Enter every booking into the Sheet from your paper notes.
  2. Mark each payment with its method.
  3. File any incidents you noted.
  4. Keep the paper until the Master confirms it is all in.
If only the internet is downPARTIAL
  1. The schedule will not refresh — use your paper notes for today.
  2. The Staff Hub and checklist still work; they are on this device.
  3. Do not guess at bookings from memory. Write, then reconcile.
