F.A.E. FACILITY · CUSTODIAN PLAYBOOK

Always
session-ready.

The custodian keeps the court and the whole facility clean, safe, and ready — so a coach never arrives to a court they have to fix first, and no parent ever sees a dirty restroom. Read this once, keep the Quick Card in the storeroom.

Trained and signed off the F.A.E. Way by the Head Ops Manager.

01

The one rule

The court is ready before the first person arrives, and clean after the last one leaves.

Everything else follows from this. If a session is starting and the court is not ready, that is the one thing that must never happen. A spill or hazard during play is stopped the moment you see it — safety does not wait for a break.

02

What you decide, what you don't

CUSTODIAN DECIDESDOES NOT DECIDE — ASK / REPORT
What to clean, in what order, and how — to keep everything ready.Booking or moving a session — that's the desk and coaches.
Making a spill or hazard safe immediately.Fixing electricals, rings, or fixtures — report damage, don't repair what isn't yours to.
Flagging when supplies are running low.Buying supplies or picking the supplier — Admin / Head Ops.

03

The day, start to finish

StartBEFORE FIRST SESSION
  1. Walk the court: floor clean and dry, nothing left out, lights/fans working.
  2. Check restrooms — clean, stocked, working.
  3. Check the drinks / common area is tidy and set.
  4. Report anything broken to the desk before players arrive.
DuringALL DAY
  1. Keep the court and common areas clean between sessions.
  2. Deal with any spill or hazard the moment you see it.
  3. Restock restrooms and drinks as they run down.
  4. Stay reachable — a coach may need the court reset fast.
CloseEND OF DAY
  1. Deep clean the court floor after the last session.
  2. Clean restrooms, empty all bins.
  3. Lock-up readiness: nothing left out, everything off that should be.
  4. Tell the next day what is running low.

04

When you're free, do this

Custodian standing list — general cleaning, do these without being told
  • Work the general-cleaning list — the deeper jobs there isn't time for mid-session.
  • Wipe down equipment, benches, rails, glass.
  • Restock everything that's low before it runs out.
  • Check the next session's court and set it up early.
  • Ask the desk or Head Ops: "I'm free — what would help most right now?"

05

When to escalate

NOW

A spill or hazard on a court in use; anything that could injure a player.

TODAY

A broken fixture, a leak, a restroom out of order, damage you can't make safe.

THIS WEEK

Supplies running consistently short; equipment wearing out; a recurring mess source.
