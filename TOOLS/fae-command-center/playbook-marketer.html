You are the face of Filamelite Basketball and F.A.E. Court online. Every session gets captured and posted, every schedule gets announced, and no inquiry waits. When a parent checks our page, it looks alive. Read this once, keep the Quick Card on your phone.

Trained and signed off the F.A.E. Way by the Head Ops Manager.

01

The one rule

Every session gets an opening and a closing story — and no inquiry waits.

Two things carry the brand: showing every session is happening, and answering people fast. A message left sitting is a booking we might lose. A session with no story is a session the world never saw. If you do nothing else, do these two.

02

What you decide, what you don't

MARKETER DECIDESDOES NOT DECIDE — ASK ABOVE
What to post, when, and how it looks — within the F.A.E. brand.Rates, promos, discounts, or announcing a price — Owner / Head Ops.
Answering routine inquiries about schedules, sessions, sign-ups.Anything you're unsure of — confirm before you reply publicly.
Which clips and photos to feature.Posting anything with player names, pay, or supplier cost — never.
Brand & privacy

Stay on brand — navy, red, gold; clean and proud. Never post supplier costs, coach pay, a player's private info, or a wrong price. A wrong price or date public is an emergency fix — take it down and correct it at once.

03

The day, start to finish

MorningOPEN THE DAY
  1. Announce the rent schedules (in the morning, or posted the night before).
  2. Clear overnight FB Messenger — basketball and volleyball accounts.
  3. Post for every account and page. A court story for every renter.
SessionsCAPTURE & POST
  1. Opening story when a session starts, closing story when it ends.
  2. Capture highlights; keep answering Messenger as it comes.
  3. Coordinate with coaches on what they filmed — collect their captures.
CloseWRAP THE DAY
  1. Post the RECAP, POG (Player of the Game), and highlights.
  2. Send / schedule tomorrow's reminders to the BATCH & Coaches GC.
  3. Check nothing went unanswered. Report the day's accomplishment.

04

05

When you're free, do this

Marketer standing list — do these without being told
  • Reply to and engage with comments and DMs across every page.
  • Plan and pre-build tomorrow's posts and graphics.
  • Refresh highlight reels and keep the best content pinned.
  • Check every scheduled post actually went out.
  • Ask Head Ops: "I'm free — what would help most right now?"

06

When to escalate

NOW

A wrong price or date went public; a bad review or public complaint; anything that could embarrass the brand.

TODAY

An inquiry you can't answer without a decision (custom price, special request); a sensitive DM.

THIS WEEK

Content falling behind; a page under-performing; a recurring question worth a pinned answer.
