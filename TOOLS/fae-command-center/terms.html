1Using the facility

The house rules. Everyone entering agrees to these.

Who may use the courts

  • Court time must be booked and paid for at the front desk before play begins.
  • Anyone entering the building signs in. Children are signed in under a responsible adult.
  • We may refuse entry or end a session for unsafe behaviour, intoxication, abusive conduct, or refusal to follow staff instructions. Court fees for a session ended this way are not refunded.

Conduct

  • Proper court shoes only. No street shoes, no bare feet.
  • No smoking, vaping, alcohol or illegal drugs anywhere on the premises.
  • Treat staff, other players and equipment with respect. Damage caused deliberately or through reckless behaviour will be charged to the person responsible or, for a child, to the adult who signed them in.
  • Outside food and drink may be restricted. Clean up after your session.

Bookings, payment and cancellation

  • Rates are posted at the front desk and in the Command Center. Member and non-member rates differ.
  • A session begins at the booked time whether or not your group has arrived.
  • Equipment borrowed (balls, scoreboard) must be returned in the condition it was issued.
  • If we cancel a booking for a reason within our control, you may rebook or be refunded.

Personal belongings

Bring valuables at your own risk. We do not operate a secured cloakroom and we are not responsible for items lost or left behind, except where the loss is caused by our own fault.

2Waiver and assumption of risk

Read this part properly. It concerns injury.

Sport carries risk

Basketball, volleyball and pickleball involve running, jumping, sudden changes of direction and contact with other players, the floor, the ball and the equipment. Injuries happen in these sports even when everything is done correctly. Those risks include, and are not limited to: sprains, fractures, dislocations, cuts, dental and eye injuries, concussion, heat exhaustion, cardiac events, and in rare cases permanent disability or death.

By entering and playing, you accept those inherent risks for yourself and for any child you sign in.

You confirm that

  • You (and any child you sign in) are physically fit to take part, and are not playing against medical advice.
  • You will stop and tell staff if you feel unwell, dizzy, or are injured.
  • You will follow safety instructions from staff and posted signage.
  • You are responsible for your own health insurance. We do not provide medical cover.

What you release

You agree not to hold F.A.E. Sports Management Services, its owners, staff and contractors liable for injury, illness, loss or damage that arises from the inherent risks of the sport, from your own actions, or from the actions of other players.

What this waiver does NOT do — and cannot do. Under Philippine law a waiver cannot excuse us from our own negligence, from a failure to keep the premises reasonably safe, or from any wilful or grossly negligent act. Nothing you sign here removes those obligations from us, or removes your right to make a claim about them. Any part of this waiver that a court finds invalid is severed and the rest continues to apply.

Emergency medical treatment

If you or a child you signed in is injured and cannot give consent at the time, you authorise our staff to arrange first aid and, where reasonably necessary, transport to a hospital or clinic. You agree to be responsible for the cost of that treatment and transport. We will contact your emergency contact as soon as we are able.

3Children and young people

Rules that apply when someone under 18 is in the building.

A child cannot sign for themselves. Only a parent or legal guardian — or an adult that parent has authorised in writing — may register a child, accept these terms on their behalf, and consent to us holding their details.

The adult who signs a child in confirms that they

  • are the child's parent or legal guardian, or have that person's authority to act for the child;
  • accept the waiver in section 2 on the child's behalf;
  • consent to us holding the child's details as described in section 4;
  • will remain responsible for that child while they are on the premises.

Supervision

  • Children under 12 must have a responsible adult in the building at all times.
  • Children aged 12 to 17 may play unaccompanied only if a parent or guardian has registered them and given a contact number that is reachable for the whole session.
  • A child is signed out to the adult who signed them in, or to someone that adult has named. If you want someone else to collect your child, tell the front desk.
  • Our staff supervise court use and safety. They are not childminders and cannot take custody of a child.

Photographs and video of children

Separate, optional, and yours to refuse. We only photograph or film a child for posting if the parent or guardian has ticked the photo permission — and that permission is a separate tick from the one that lets us hold their details. Saying no changes nothing about the child's use of the facility.

  • You may withdraw photo permission at any time. Tell the front desk and we stop, and remove what we still control.
  • We do not publish a child's full name, school, or any contact detail alongside their image.
  • Other customers taking photos are not under our control. Tell staff immediately if someone is photographing children and we will deal with it.
  • CCTV, where installed, runs for security and is covered in section 4. It is not the same as photo permission.

Safeguarding

If staff have a genuine concern that a child is being harmed or is at risk, we may report it to the proper authorities. We will do that regardless of any consent given here, because child protection law requires it.

Our lawful basis

  • Your consent — for holding your contact details, for a child's details, and separately for photographs.
  • Contract — to take a booking and issue a receipt.
  • Legal obligation — tax and business records; incident reports; child protection reporting.
  • Legitimate interests — knowing who is in the building for safety and evacuation, and protecting the premises. We keep this to what is proportionate.

Who can see it

  • Front desk staff see names, the adult-and-child link, and check-in status — what they need to run the door safely. They do not see the full register.

  • Emergency services and medical staff, if you are injured.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell your details, and we do not share them with advertisers.

Where it is kept

Records are held on the facility's own office computer and its backups. They are not published online. Access is restricted by role, and every deletion is recorded.

5Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act these are yours, free of charge. A parent exercises them on behalf of their child.
  • Be informed — this notice.
  • Access — ask for a copy of what we hold about you and your children.
  • Correct it — have anything wrong fixed.
  • Object — tell us to stop processing, where processing is based on consent.
  • Erasure or blocking — have your record deleted, unless we must keep it for a legal reason such as tax records or an open incident.
  • Data portability — get your record in a file you can take elsewhere.
  • Damages — claim compensation for a violation.
  • Complain — to us first, and to the National Privacy Commission if we do not resolve it.

Withdrawing consent is easy and has no penalty. Tell the front desk. We will delete your record, including your children's, unless the law requires us to keep a specific part of it — and we will tell you which part and why. We aim to act on any request within 15 working days.
