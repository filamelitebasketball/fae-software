ACCESS & ROLES

Who can see what. Master (you & your wife) sees all figures. Front desk never sees money. Admin is your trusted middle — set their PIN and whether they may see money.
Master PIN is 2021 and is fixed in the code. This gate stops staff seeing figures — it is not security; anyone opening the file in a text editor can read the data.

ONLINE SYNC

Where worker submissions auto-send when online. Leave blank to keep everything on-device (QR hand-off still works). The endpoint must accept a JSON POST — a Supabase REST URL, a Google Apps Script web app, or a Lovable edge function.

DATA PRIVACY

The visitor register holds children's names, ages and sometimes health notes. Under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) the business must name someone responsible, tell people how long records are kept, and actually delete them on time. These settings appear in the public privacy notice (terms.html) and drive the retention tools on the Visitors tab.
If you ever hold records for 1,000 people or more, the National Privacy Commission expects you to register as a personal information controller. Court rentals reach that number faster than owners expect — check the count on the Visitors tab.

INTERFACE

How much the screens move. Full is the upgraded look — panels slide in, numbers count up, buttons respond to a tap. Subtle keeps only fades. Off is completely still, best on an old front-desk PC. A phone or PC set to "reduce motion" in its own accessibility settings is always respected, whatever is picked here.
