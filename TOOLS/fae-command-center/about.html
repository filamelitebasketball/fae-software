ABOUT US

Who we are

Fil-Am Elite Management (F.A.E.) is a sports development organization founded by Coach Junior Raymundo, built around one belief: young athletes deserve real coaching and a place to play that is run properly. We operate Filamelite Basketball, our volleyball program, and F.A.E. Court — bringing structured training, a well-kept facility, and organized competition to the community across our Lipa and Malolos branches.

What sets us apart is not just the game — it is how we run the operation. Every coach and staff member is trained and signed off to a set standard before they work with a single player or parent. When you train or play with F.A.E., you are getting a team that takes it seriously.
