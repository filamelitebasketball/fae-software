=====================
Version: console v1.0 - 2026-09-03

(This replaces "READ ME FIRST.txt". The old one is still there so you can
 compare. Delete it once you are happy with this version.)


HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
Double-click  START-COMMAND-CENTER.bat

A black window opens, then the Command Center appears in your browser.
LEAVE THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN while you work. Closing it turns live mode off.

Look at the chip in the top-left of the page:

  GREEN "LIVE"        Reading the court schedule straight from the Google
                      Sheet. The four tiles at the top are real and current.

  GOLD "LOCAL ONLY"   You opened the page directly instead of using the
                      launcher. Everything still works except those four
                      tiles. Close it and use the .bat file.

If you would rather not use the launcher, double-click console.html. You just
will not get the live status tiles.


WHAT YOU SEE FIRST
------------------
The top of the home page answers the questions people actually ask, without
you clicking anything:

  COURT RIGHT NOW ... in use (and by whom) or free
  NEXT BOOKING ...... what time, and who
  BOOKED TODAY ...... how many bookings today
  EXPECTED TODAY .... the pesos those bookings should bring in

Underneath: today's full schedule, who is on shift, and your daily checklist.


THE TWO BUTTONS THAT MATTER MOST
--------------------------------
  TODAY'S SCHEDULE  ->  who is booked, when, which court. Check it first
                        every day and before every renter.

  LOG AN INCIDENT   ->  the moment anything goes wrong (no-show, damage,
                        injury, argument, overtime), press this and record
                        it with a photo. Logging it right away is what stops
                        the mix-ups and the missing records.

You do not need the person to be a saved member. Type their name in the
name box, add a title, attach a photo, file it. Keep photos small and few.


KEYBOARD SHORTCUT
-----------------
The bar at the bottom of the screen jumps straight to any page. Click it,
type any of these, press Enter:

  schedule   incident   staff   payroll   dashboard
  nxgen      playbook   rates   sheet     members

THE MOST IMPORTANT THING TO UNDERSTAND
--------------------------------------
We run on several machines. Not everything is shared between them.

  SAME ON EVERY DEVICE
    - The court schedule (the Google Sheet)
    - Members, payments, roles, stock (the live site's admin page)

  SAVED ON THIS MACHINE ONLY
    - Your checklist ticks
    - Staff Hub shifts, tasks and drinks counts
    - Payroll data
    - Dashboard records and incident drafts

On the home page, every number carries a small coloured label telling you
which kind it is. Blue and green travel between devices. Gold does not.

  THE RULE: if it must match on every device, it does not live in this
  folder. Put it on the Sheet or on the live site.

Export a backup from the Staff Hub regularly. A machine that dies takes its
local data with it.
