F.A.E. MANAGEMENT · ADMIN PLAYBOOK

The books
stay honest.

Admin is the back-office partner to the court attendant. You keep the money count honest and the records straight — so that at any moment, what the business believes is true actually is. Read this once, keep the Quick Card at your desk.

Trained and signed off the F.A.E. Way by the Head Ops Manager.

01

The one rule

The COH must match the record before anyone goes home — every day.

COH = Cash On Hand. At end of day the cash in the drawer must equal what was marked paid on the Sheet. You and the court attendant count it together, it matches, you file it, and you report it. A day that does not reconcile is not closed — it is escalated.

02

What you decide, what you don't

ADMIN DECIDESDOES NOT DECIDE — ASK ABOVE
Correcting a record when you have proof (receipt, log, message).Rates, discounts, refunds — Owner only.
Chasing and confirming supplier orders (uniform, medals, photographer).Re-opening a locked day, adjusting someone's hours — Head Ops.
Preparing certificate name lists and batch rosters.Anything legal, contractual, or about staff pay — Owner.

03

The day, start to finish

StartFIRST THING
  1. Open the schedule and the income sheet. Confirm yesterday's COH is reconciled and filed.
  2. Check the ADMIN group chat for what needs doing today.
  3. Note today's deadlines — orders, certificates, batch cut-offs.
DuringALL DAY
  1. Keep records current as things happen — never in one batch at night.
  2. Follow up suppliers: uniform, medals, photographer. Confirm dates.
  3. Prepare certificate name lists (every 12th session) and keep batch rosters correct.
  4. Coordinate cleaning/orders with the custodian via the GC.
CloseEND OF DAY
  1. Reconcile COH with the court attendant — cash matches marked payments.
  2. File the day's count. Keep the paper trail.
  3. Report COH and the day's accomplishment to Head Ops / Owner.
  4. Hand over anything unfinished to tomorrow, in writing.
If the COH does not match

Do not adjust the number to make it fit, and do not wait for tomorrow. Note the amount and what you think happened, tell Head Ops before you leave. An honest short count is a small problem; a hidden one is a big one.

04

When you're free, do this

Admin standing list — do these without being told
  • Chase any supplier order that isn't confirmed yet.
  • Tidy and back up records; fix any gaps you spot.
  • Prepare the next batch's name lists and sizes early.
  • Confirm player names and uniform sizes before the supplier cut-off.
  • Ask Head Ops: "I'm free — what would help most right now?"

05

When to escalate

NOW

COH does not match by any amount you cannot explain; money missing.

TODAY

A supplier delay that puts a session, batch, or tournament at risk; a disputed payment.

THIS WEEK

Records that keep going out of sync; a recurring gap in a process.
