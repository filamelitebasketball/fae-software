Turns a pickleball match video into a highlights video automatically: every rally in one video,
the longest rally and the full game, ready for phones (vertical 9:16 or landscape 16:9).


START IT
1. Double-click DinkCutIO-Trial.exe. Nothing to install. Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (The app is new and not yet known to Windows. This only happens once.)
3. An "Opening..." window appears first. The first start can take up to 30 seconds.


MAKE HIGHLIGHTS
1. Home page: under Match video, click Browse and pick a match recording.
2. Click "Make highlights" (right side). The % bar shows progress.
3. The videos are saved in your Videos folder, under "DinkCut IO".
   A QR code appears: scan it with a phone on the same WiFi to download the videos.
   If Windows asks about the firewall, allow "Private networks" so phones can download.


BEST RESULTS
- A fixed camera that sees the whole court, with its microphone on
  (the app listens for the paddle hits and watches for movement on the court).
- One court per video.
- Missing rallies? Detection (left menu) > raise Sensitivity. Extra rallies from the
  next court? Lower Sensitivity or raise "Ignore edges %".
