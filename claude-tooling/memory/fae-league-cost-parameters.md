Fil-Am Elite runs a 10-team basketball league (Philippines, pesos). Operating rates given by the user on 31 Aug 2026:

- Court rent: ₱800/hour
- Tournament games: 12-minute quarters, 1.5-hour slot
- Referees: 2 per game. Standard ₱1,000, bargained to ₱900 "per game" on a season contract
- Photographer: 4.5-5 hrs coverage, quoted ₱2,000-3,000, plan at ₱3,000
- Weekday "open runs": ₱200 per player, 10-minute quarters, 1-hour games, referee cost pro-rated down, no photographer

**Two questions still unanswered** (both move the total a lot):
1. Is the ₱900 referee fee per referee, or does it cover both? Model assumes per referee (₱1,800/game); the alternative halves the largest cost line.
2. Does the photographer bill ₱3,000 per 5-hour block or flat per play date? A 5-game day is 7.5 hrs, so the block reading doubles it.

Also unstated: paying players per team (assumed 12) and open-run nights per week (assumed 1).
