---
description: Open the NXGEN Stat Command Center dashboard for game-night stat entry and management
---

Launch the NXGEN Stat Command Center widget dashboard. This renders an interactive stat entry grid with:
- Game selector (week, game, division, status)
- Editable player stat grids for home and away teams
- Live-computed team totals, game leader, completion tracking
- Tabs for stat entry, leaders, standings, and audit log
- Action buttons to push to Supabase, export Excel, and recalculate

Use the `show_widget` tool from the `mcp__visualize` toolset to render the dashboard inline. Follow the template in `skills/stat-command-center/references/dashboard-widget.md`.
