## 4. Start here — the cover photo & profile picture

Your page's first impression is the profile picture and cover photo. Get these right before you post anything, because every reveal caption below assumes they're ready.

### Profile picture (your logo)

- **What it is:** your NXGEN logo, clean and centered on a solid or subtle grayscale background (matches your website's grayscale look).
- **Size:** upload **500 × 500 px** (square). Facebook displays it as a circle at ~170 px, so keep the logo centered and inside a safe circle — don't let text touch the edges.
- **Rule:** it must be readable at thumbnail size. If your logo has small text, use a simplified mark for the profile and save the full logo for the cover.

### Cover photo (your billboard)

- **What it is:** the league name + motto "Where legacies are born" + a strong basketball image, ideally hinting at the four divisions or the live-tech angle (a stat overlay, a scoreboard, a court under lights).
- **Size:** upload **851 × 315 px** (or larger at the same 2.7:1 ratio, e.g. 1640 × 624, for a crisp retina look).
- **Mobile safe zone:** Facebook crops the sides on mobile and the top/bottom on desktop. Keep the logo, name, and motto in the **center**, roughly the middle 640 px, so nothing important gets cut.
- **Include:** logo, "NXGEN Premier League," the motto, and — if it fits cleanly — your website URL in a corner.

**Design tip:** keep it grayscale-forward to match your site, with one accent color for energy. Consistency between the page and the website makes the whole thing feel legit and built-out — which it is.

## 8. The 2-week launch calendar

Compressed and high-frequency because you're ~2 weeks out. Roughly one feed post per day plus Stories. Post in the **evening (7–9pm Philippine time)** when your audience is off work/school. Times below are day markers, not rigid — the sequence matters more than the exact hour.

### Phase 1 — Identity (Days 1–3): "We're here."

- **Day 1 — Profile picture reveal.** Use a Section 5 caption. Get the logo live. Ask people to follow and tag two teammates.
- **Day 2 — Cover photo reveal + pinned intro.** Use a Section 6 caption. Pin it. This is your anchor post.
- **Day 3 — Vision & mission post.** Share the vision/mission (Section 2) as a clean graphic or text-on-image. Caption: *"This is what we're building — and why. Where legacies are born."* Drop the four divisions.

### Phase 2 — The hook (Days 4–8): "Here's why we're different."

One pillar per day. This is where you sell the platform before you ask for anything.

- **Day 4 — Live stats.** Screenshot/mockup of the leaderboards or player rankings page. *"Your numbers follow you. Every point, rebound, and assist — tracked live. [link]"*
- **Day 5 — Division spotlight: Rising Stars & Legacy.** Explain who each is for. Tag the age/level. Drive to the site.
- **Day 6 — Live coverage.** *"Can't make it courtside? Watch every game live. Real coverage, real time."* Point to where streams/gallery will live.
- **Day 7 — Division spotlight: 3x3 & King of the Court.** The fast, dramatic formats — great for shareable hype.
- **Day 8 — Live schedule/feed + website tour.** A short screen-recording or carousel walking through the site: schedule, standings, rankings, register. *"Everything in one place, always up to date. This is how we run — for us and for you."*

### Phase 3 — Open the doors (Days 9–12): "Register now."

Now that the "why" has landed, make the ask directly and repeatedly.

- **Day 9 — Registration is OPEN.** Big announcement. Clear steps: how to register, deadline, cost (if any), which divisions. CTA button + link. *"Registration is officially open. Claim your spot. Where legacies are born. 👉 [register link]"*
- **Day 10 — Coaches & teams.** Speak to them directly: roster management, live data as a coaching tool, how to enter a team. *"Coaches — bring your squad. Track your players' growth game by game."*
- **Day 11 — Sponsors / partners.** One post pitching reach + the professional tech angle. *"Partner with a league built different. Live audiences, real data, real community. DM us."* (Also DM/email prospects directly — don't rely on the post alone.)
- **Day 12 — Social proof / FAQ.** Answer the top questions (who can join, when, where, cost, how stats work). Reshare early registrations, screenshots of interest, or a countdown graphic.

### Phase 4 — Countdown (Days 13–14): "It's here."

- **Day 13 — Final call.** *"Last days to register before Season 1 tips off. Don't watch from the sidelines. [link]"* Add urgency: registration deadline.
- **Day 14 — Launch / first game day.** Announce the schedule going live, first fixtures, where to watch. *"Season 1 starts now. The legacy begins. Full schedule live: [link]"* Go live on the page if you can — even a phone stream of the first tip-off.

**Between feed posts, run Stories daily:** countdown stickers, behind-the-scenes, "X days left," reshare anyone who tags you, polls ("Which division are you entering?"). Stories keep you at the top of the feed without cluttering the timeline.

## 10. What to track (keep it simple)

Check these weekly in Facebook's Insights — don't drown in metrics:

- **Page follows** — are you building an audience?
- **Post reach & shares** — which posts travel? (Shares matter most; they're free reach.) Do more of what gets shared.
- **Link clicks to the website** — is the content actually driving people to register?
- **Registrations** — the only number that truly counts. Tie it back: which posts drove sign-ups?

If a post gets shared a lot, boost it with a small budget (even ₱300–500) targeted to your city + basketball interests. Boost proven winners, never cold guesses.
