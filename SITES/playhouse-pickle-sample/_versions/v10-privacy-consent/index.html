Privacy notice

Last updated September 26, 2026

Who we are. Playhouse Pickle, Funnside Ningnangan, Bacoor Blvd., Molino, Bacoor, Cavite, is responsible for your personal data on this site and at our courts.

What we collect. Your name and email when you sign up, and the mobile and skill level you add to your profile; the email you type at the courtside kiosk; your bookings, payments and WiFi vouchers; and video of the court while a recording runs.

Why. To run your bookings, payments, account and WiFi, and to send your full game and highlights. Offers and open play news only if you tick the box for them; turn them off any time in your profile.

Recordings. The overhead camera records only between start and stop on the courtside tablet. Everyone on that court is in the video, so agree as a group before you tap start. Your links go only to the email you give. Videos are deleted after 30 days unless you keep them, and we never post a video without your permission. To play unrecorded or have a video deleted early, ask the front desk.

Who else sees it. Our staff, and the providers that host this site, store the videos and send the emails, only to run this service. The payment app you choose (GCash or Maya) handles your payment. We never sell your data.

How long. Account data until you delete your account. Kiosk emails without an opt-in, 30 days. Payment records as long as the law requires.

Your rights. You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, object to its use, withdraw your consent, or claim damages, and you can complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). Players under 18 need a parent or guardian to agree for them.

Contact. Front desk, 0966 875 2617, or message facebook.com/playhousepickle. Data protection officer: to be named by the owners.
