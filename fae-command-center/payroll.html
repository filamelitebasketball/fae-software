⚙️ Statutory Rates
Set the Regional Minimum Wage to the rate in the current DOLE wage order for your region (F.A.E. is in CALABARZON / Region IV-A) and the compliance panel will flag anyone paid below it. Wage orders are revised periodically — this is left at 0 until you enter the figure in force, so no outdated number is assumed.
⚖️ The Rules This Applies
Normal hours — Art. 83. Eight hours a day. Art. 85 requires a meal period of at least 60 minutes for work of more than 5 hours, and that break is not compensable. So a ₱500 day for "9 hours on site" is 8 paid working hours plus an unpaid meal → hourly rate = ₱500 ÷ 8 = ₱62.50. Shifts of 5 hours or less have no meal deducted.

Overtime — Art. 87. Work beyond 8 hours earns the hourly rate plus at least 25% on an ordinary day → ₱78.13/hr. Overtime on a rest day or holiday carries an extra 30% on that day's premium rate.

Undertime not offset — Art. 88. Undertime on one day cannot be charged against overtime on another. Each day is computed on its own here, so this never happens.

Night differential — Art. 86. Every hour worked between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM earns an extra 10% of the applicable hourly rate. Night hours that are also overtime get 10% of the overtime rate, not the base rate.

Weekly rest day — Art. 91. One rest day after every 6 consecutive normal workdays. The compliance panel flags any run of 7 or more straight days.

Rest day & holiday premiums — Arts. 93–94.
  Ordinary 100% · OT 125%
  rest / special 130% · OT 169%
  rest+special 150% · OT 195%
  holiday 200% · OT 260%
  rest+holiday 260% · OT 338%
  holiday-unworked 100% of the daily wage for an unworked regular holiday — this is owed even though nobody reported for work.

13th month pay — PD 851. Total basic salary for the year ÷ 12, excluding OT, night differential and premiums. Due on or before December 24. Tracked on the Employees tab.

Service incentive leave — Art. 95. 5 days with pay per year once an employee completes one year of service, convertible to cash if unused. Not computed here — track it separately and settle it at year-end.

Scope. This computes wages under the Labor Code. It is not legal advice, and it does not compute withholding tax (BIR) — confirm your figures with your accountant or the DOLE regional office before releasing pay.
