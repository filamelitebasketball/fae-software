F.A.E. COMMAND CENTER
=====================

HOW TO USE
----------
1. Open this folder.
2. Double-click  index.html
3. That's your home base. Everything opens from there.

You (or anyone at the front desk) only ever need to remember ONE thing:
open index.html. It works offline. No internet or login needed.


WHAT'S INSIDE
-------------
index.html ........ THE HOME PAGE. Open this one. Live clock, today's date,
                    the daily front-desk checklist, and buttons to everything.

schedule.html ..... LIVE court schedule — a direct, always-current view of your
                    Google Sheet renter calendar. Click "Open to edit" to change it.

dashboard.html .... The full business dashboard — incidents, revenue, renters,
                    payments, payroll, inventory. (Its schedule page now shows the
                    same live Google Sheet at the top.)

nxgen-stats.html .. NXGEN basketball league stat entry, leaders & standings.

assets/ ........... Logos used by the home page. Leave this folder alone.


THE TWO BUTTONS THAT MATTER MOST
--------------------------------
- "Today's Court Schedule"  -> shows who is booked, when, and which court.
                               Check this first every day so there's no
                               confusion about the day or the time slot.

- "Log an Incident"  -> the moment anything goes wrong (no-show, damage,
                        injury, argument, overtime), press this and record it
                        with a photo. Logging it right away is what stops the
                        mix-ups and missing records.


DAILY CHECKLIST
---------------
The home page has a 5-step checklist. Tick each step as you go through the
day. It resets by itself every new morning, so you always start fresh.


LIVE WEBSITES
-------------
The two "Live Websites" boxes on the home page open your real sites:
  - F.A.E. Management  -> https://fae-court-connect.lovable.app
  - NXGEN Premier League -> https://nxgenpremierleague.lovable.app
These are the SHARED, online source of truth (they sync across devices). The
local dashboard.html is a fast single-device tool; for records that everyone
must see (members, payments), use the live site's admin panel.

LOGGING A WALK-IN INCIDENT
--------------------------
On "Log an Incident" you no longer need the person to be a saved member. Just
type their name in the "name / renter" box, add a title, attach a photo, and
file it. Keep photos to a few small ones so it saves reliably.


TIP: Right-click index.html -> Send to -> Desktop (create shortcut)
     so it's one click away.
