📜 When a renter arrives

Follow these in order, every single time. This is what stops the mix-ups.
Open the Live Court Schedule first

Before anything else, look at today's column. Never take someone's word for their booking.

Match the person to the booking

Name, court, start time, end time. If they are not on the schedule, they are not booked — take the booking properly before they step on the court.

Say the end time out loud

“You're on Court 1 until 4 PM.” Saying it prevents the overtime argument later.

Collect payment now, not later

Cash or GCash. Mark it the moment it is in your hand. Basketball ₱900/hr · Volleyball ₱750/hr · Pickleball ₱400/hr.

Check the court before they start

Lights, nets, floor, rubbish. If something is already broken, log it now so it is not blamed on this group.

Watch the clock near the end

Give a 10-minute warning. If they run over, that is overtime — log it as an incident and charge it.

Log anything that goes wrong, immediately

No-show, damage, injury, argument, overtime. A logged incident is a solved incident.

Hand over cleanly

Before you leave: tasks submitted, drinks counted, cash matches, next shift told what happened.
